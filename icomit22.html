WELCOME TO ICOMIT 2022

We are exceptionally pleased to announce the 2022 1st International Conference On Multidisciplinary Applications of Information Technology (ICOMIT). This conference will attract authors from various disciplines to share their latest research findings and ideas.

Due to the COVID-19 pandemic restrictions on movement, the 1st ICOMIT conference will be held virtual (online). Delegates and other conference attendees will be given the virtual conference link in due course. Organized by the Faculty of Computer Science, Universitas Brawijaya, the Conference will be held on July 18-19, 2022.

About ICOMIT 2022

Pandemic Covid-19 has changed people's habits and how industries perform their business. However, we also have seen academia, researchers, graduate students, business entrepreneurs, practitioners, innovators, representatives of indigenous peoples and faith communities, and policymakers bring innovations through information technology to their fields and localities to thrive despite the challenges brought by the pandemic.

The International Conference on Multidisciplinary Applications of Information Technology (ICOMIT) brings together authors who bring forward innovative ideas, visions, dreams of solutions by applying information technology in their respective disciplines. Qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods research studies from university scholars and researchers are accepted. Case studies, lessons learned, success stories, and best practices from policy makers, practitioners, religious leaders, and indigenous peoples are also accepted. Authors are more than welcome to use the occasion to share their expertise and understanding of technology utilization to achieve better living in the post-pandemic Covid-19.

The theme for ICOMIT 2022 will be "Multidisciplinary Applications of Information Technology for Better Living in the Post-Pandemic Era."

ICOMIT 2022 will be held in the Faculty of Computer Science, Universitas Brawijaya, Malang, Jawa Timur, Indonesia, on July 18-19, 2022.

Submission

Authors are invited to submit full paper in English through EDAS. All submitted manuscript will undergo review process for acceptance decision.
