WELCOME TO ICOMIT 2024

We are exceptionally pleased to announce the 2024 3rd International Conference On Multidisciplinary Applications of Information Technology (ICOMIT). This conference will attract authors from various disciplines to share their latest research findings and ideas.

Call For Paper

About ICOMIT 2024

Global progress refers to the advancement of humanity as a whole towards a better, more prosperous, and sustainable future. However, we also have seen academia, researchers, graduate students, business entrepreneurs, practitioners, innovators, representatives of indigenous peoples and faith communities, and policymakers bring innovations through information technology to their fields and localities to thrive despite the challenges brought by the pandemic.

The International Conference on Multidisciplinary Applications of Information Technology (ICOMIT) brings together authors who bring forward innovative ideas, visions, dreams of solutions by applying information technology in their respective disciplines. Qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods research studies from university scholars and researchers are accepted. Case studies, lessons learned, success stories, and best practices from policy makers, practitioners, religious leaders, and indigenous peoples are also accepted. Author are encouraged to share their expertise and understanding of technology utilization to improve various aspects of human life across the world, such as economic growth, social development, technological advancement, and environmental sustainability.

The theme for ICOMIT 2024 will be "Secure and Intelligent Digital Transformation".

ICOMIT 2024 will be held at Hotel Aruss, Semarang, Central Java, Indonesia, on July 22-23, 2024.

Submission

Authors are invited to submit full paper in English through EDAS. All submitted manuscript will undergo review process for acceptance decision.

Accepted high quality papers will be proposed to be published as a chapter by book series of the renowned publisher SPRINGER NATURE (Proposal under consideration).

All chapters published in the series are submitted for consideration in the Web of Science of Clarivate Analytics (previously known as ISI).
